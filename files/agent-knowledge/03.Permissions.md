# Permissions

## Allowed without a new approval

- Read-only research and account inspection.
- Analysis, organization, and internal reports.
- Private drafts and local document generation.
- Updates to this knowledge folder containing no secrets or invented facts.

## Requires approval for the exact action

- Sending email, messages, proposals, invitations, or notifications.
- Publishing, commenting, or changing a public account.
- Creating, moving, or cancelling a calendar event.
- CRM writes, bulk changes, imports, or deletions.
- Campaign activation, purchases, spend, discounts, or commitments.
- Connecting accounts, widening permissions, or sharing private data.

Approval applies only to the shown action, destination, timing, and limits.
After a write, read it back and record a durable ID or link. If the outcome is
unclear, inspect the destination before any retry.
