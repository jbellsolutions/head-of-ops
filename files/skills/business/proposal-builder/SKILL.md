# Proposal Builder

Read the business context, discovery notes, desired outcome, scope, price,
timing, responsibilities, assumptions, exclusions, proof, and next step. Mark
anything missing as `unknown`; never invent commercial terms.

Build in this order:

1. One-page decision summary.
2. Current situation in the recipient's language.
3. Desired outcome and measurable success criteria.
4. Recommended plan and sequence.
5. Deliverables, responsibilities, timeline, and dependencies.
6. Investment and payment terms exactly as approved.
7. Assumptions, exclusions, expiry, and next step.

Create a private Markdown source first, then DOCX or PDF when useful. PandaDoc
is the connected system of record. A private unsent test draft is allowed.
Before sending or requesting signatures, show the exact recipients, title,
price, terms, expiry, and message and obtain approval. After sending, read back
the document ID, status, recipients, and link. Reconcile before retrying.
